About the Practice

Restoring the healing potential within each individual.

Body Balance Therapies has been a trusted source of effective bodywork and massage therapy for many years. We believe therapeutic touch, professionally applied in one of its many established forms, has the power to restore and activate the healing potential present in each of us.

Sessions are led by Kim Walker — professionally trained, licensed and certified in her modalities, with training across both Eastern and Western disciplines. Clients come for everything from sports injuries and ongoing conditions to the strain of day-to-day living.
